Authorize Apple Music

A one-time step that lets the site read your recently played tracks. Everything on this page runs in your browser; the private key is used locally to sign a token and is never sent anywhere except, as a signature, to Apple.

1. Developer key

2. Your Music User Token

Save this as the APPLE_MUSIC_USER_TOKEN secret in the GitHub repository. It lasts about six months; when the Action starts failing with a 403, come back here and make a new one.

Where these come from

  1. In the Apple Developer portal, open Certificates, Identifiers & Profiles → Keys and create a key with MusicKit enabled. Download the .p8 file. The Key ID is shown on that page.
  2. The Team ID is in the top right of the developer portal under your name, or under Membership details.
  3. The .p8 can only be downloaded once. Keep it somewhere safe.
